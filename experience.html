Beyond the plate

Aurum was born from a quiet rebellion against the rush. Here, we celebrate slow rituals, heritage techniques, and the poetry of unhurried conversations. Our kitchen is a canvas — seasonal, honest, deeply rooted in craft.

Led by Chef Mira Sen, each menu is a dialogue between European bistro soul and Indian terroir. From hand-rolled pasta to single-origin brews, every element is intentional. We source from over 25 local farms and artisanal producers, ensuring zero compromise on quality.

The Aurum Chronicle

2021

Genesis · Aurum opens as a hidden oasis in Bengaluru's green quarter, championing farm-to-table integrity with just 18 seats and a dream.

2022

Recognition · Awarded "Best Bistro Concept" by Epicurean India; launch of the seasonal tasting menu that sold out in 48 hours.

2023

Expansion · Artist-in-Residence program & coffee lab workshops introduced. Over 1200 guests attended our first Supper Club series.

2025

Legacy · Aurum becomes a benchmark for sustainable luxury and culinary storytelling — now a 5-star rated destination.
